# 🛍️ Modern E-Commerce Platform — Feature Catalog

A modern, fast, and feature-rich PHP & MySQL E-Commerce solution designed specifically for Indian online retail businesses and digital agencies.

---

## 🌟 Top Core Features

### 1. 💳 Integrated PhonePe & Multi-Mode Payment Gateway
* **Direct Bank Transfer:** Payments go straight into the merchant's bank account without expensive middleman fees or Shopify commissions.
* **All Indian Payment Modes Supported:** 
  - UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, QR code)
  - Credit & Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay)
  - Net Banking across 50+ Indian banks
  - Cash on Delivery (COD) with automated fraud & blacklist protection
* **Auto-Reconciliation:** Instant payment callbacks and webhook notifications verify order payments automatically in real time.

---

### 2. 📲 Dual-Mode Automated WhatsApp Notification Engine (Meta API + Fallback Direct Delivery)
* **Zero Message Rejection Risk:** 
  - Supports official **Meta Cloud API** template messages.
  - If a template name is left empty or unapproved, the system **automatically switches to the Bridge & Fallback Template Engine**, ensuring 100% guaranteed delivery as direct WhatsApp text!
* **Instant Customer Order Confirmation:** Customers immediately receive an order receipt on WhatsApp as soon as payment is made.
* **Shipping & Tracking Alerts:** Automated messages when order status changes to "Dispatched", "Out for Delivery", or "Delivered".
* **Admin New Order WhatsApp Alert:** Shop owner instantly gets a WhatsApp ping on their personal phone with customer details, order amount, address, and items ordered.
* **Smart Dynamic Variable Compiler:** Supports 15+ dynamic tags with case-insensitive single and double curly brackets (`{customer_name}`, `{order_id}`, `{order_total}`, `{order_date}`, `{payment_method}`, `{expected_delivery_date}`, `{order_link}`, etc.).
* **Abandoned Cart Recovery:** Automated reminder prompts to customers who left items in their cart without completing checkout.

---

### 3. 🚚 Real-Time Courier & Shipping Logistics Module
* **Live Order Tracking:** Customers can track their packages using their order ID and phone number.
* **Multi-Courier Support:** Ready for Indian logistics partners (Delhivery, Blue Dart, Shiprocket, DTDC, Ecom Express, Xpressbees).
* **AWB Number Tracking:** Automated tracking links generated for dispatches.
* **Custom Shipping Rates:** Flat-rate, zone-based, or free shipping rules over a specific order value.
* **COD Blacklist Guard:** Block addresses or phone numbers with a history of fake orders or high RTO.

---

### 4. 🎛️ Executive Admin Suite & UI/UX Design (36+ Modernized Modules)
* **Executive Dark Navy Theme:**
  - Ultra-modern aesthetic with dark navy hero headers, KPI stat metric cards, pastel icon badges, and high-contrast data tables.
  - **100% Mobile Responsive:** Optimized for smartphones — shop owners can manage their entire catalog, update prices, and process orders straight from their phone without horizontal scroll or button overlap.
* **Product Catalog Management:**
  - Unlimited products, categories, and subcategories with instant AJAX search.
  - Multi-image product gallery with zoom preview.
  - Sale prices, discount tags, low-stock alerts, and homepage featured product counter.
  - Bulk CSV product import and export.
* **Order Processing & Invoicing:**
  - Instant 1-click printable PDF invoices.
  - Live order status workflow (Pending, Processing, Shipped, Delivered, Cancelled).
* **Automated Social Media Marketing Hub:**
  - Integrated Social Media scheduler, multi-platform publishing queue, and dynamic caption templates.
* **WEBSEO Search Engine Suite:**
  - Configure global search meta, OpenGraph tags, page-level keyword overrides, and automatic live XML sitemap (`/sitemap.xml`) and `robots.txt` generator.
* **Storefront Theme Customizer:**
  - Fine-tune brand palettes, Google Fonts typography (Poppins, Montserrat, Roboto, Inter, etc.), and header navigation layouts with live real-time preview.
* **System Optimization & Acceleration:**
  - Reclaim database storage, prime bytecode OPcache, clear expired session garbage, and boost store TTFB response speed.

* **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript, MDBootstrap (Fast, responsive, modern aesthetics)
* **Hosting Compatibility:** CPanel, Hostinger, Shared Hosting, Cloud VPS, or Local XAMPP
* **Zero Recurring Fees:** No framework bloat, no mandatory monthly third-party subscriptions.
